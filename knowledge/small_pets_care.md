## Rabbit diet
Rabbits need unlimited fresh hay (it should be most of their diet), fresh leafy greens daily, and a small,
measured amount of pellets. Sudden diet changes can upset digestion. A rabbit that stops eating or stops producing
droppings for more than about 12 hours needs a vet urgently.
TASK: Refill hay and fresh greens | category=feeding | minutes=10 | priority=high | time=08:00 | frequency=daily
TASK: Refresh water | category=feeding | minutes=5 | priority=high | frequency=daily

## Rabbit exercise
Rabbits need several hours of supervised time outside the enclosure every day to run, jump, and explore in a
rabbit-proofed area. They are social and do best with daily interaction.
TASK: Supervised free-roam time | category=enrichment | minutes=60 | priority=high | frequency=daily

## Cage and habitat cleaning
Spot-clean litter and bedding daily and do a full clean of the enclosure weekly for rabbits, guinea pigs, and
hamsters. Guinea pigs need daily vitamin C from fresh vegetables or a supplement.
TASK: Spot-clean enclosure | category=general | minutes=10 | priority=medium | frequency=daily
TASK: Full habitat clean | category=general | minutes=30 | priority=medium | frequency=weekly

## Birds
Pet birds need fresh food and water daily, time out of the cage, and social interaction. Cover the cage at night
for 10 to 12 hours of sleep. Non-stick cookware fumes (PTFE) can be fatal to birds.
TASK: Fresh food and water for bird | category=feeding | minutes=10 | priority=high | frequency=daily
TASK: Out-of-cage social time | category=enrichment | minutes=30 | priority=medium | frequency=daily
