## Consistency
Pets thrive on routine. Keep feeding, walks, and medication at roughly the same times each day. Anchor the day
with fixed-time essentials (meals, meds, walks) and fit flexible tasks such as grooming and enrichment into gaps.

## Busy days
When time is short, protect health-critical tasks first: medication, feeding, water, and toilet breaks. Enrichment
and grooming can move to another day, but they should not be skipped for long. A short sniff walk or a 5-minute
puzzle is better than nothing.

## Multiple pets
With several pets, stagger tasks so they do not overlap, or pair compatible ones (for example, feed the cat while
the dog eats in another room). Watch for one pet's routine consistently crowding out another's.
